MULTI-AGENT PIPELINE

Grounded, Not Guessed

Most health chatbots send a question straight to an LLM. MedTwin doesn't let the model decide what a lab value means — targets come from retrieved ADA Standards of Care, NICE NG28, and WHO HEARTS-D passages, while a Random Forest model trained on NHANES data supplies the population-based risk estimate.

WHAT-IF SIMULATION

See the Effect Before It Happens

Ask "what if my HbA1c stays high?" or "what if my BMI drops?" — the simulation layer reruns the risk model on the changed value and labels the result exactly as what it is: an estimate based on population patterns, not a medical forecast.

DIGITAL TWIN PROFILE

Structured From Your Real Biomarkers

Maps your 9 core lab values directly to national NHANES distributions, computing individual percentile rankings and poor-control probability without clinical guesswork.

GUIDELINE RETRIEVAL

Grounded in ADA & NICE Standards

Retrieves verified clinical targets from ADA 2026 Standards of Care, NICE NG28, and WHO HEARTS-D, ensuring every clinical claim is backed by peer-reviewed evidence.

VERIFICATION ENGINE

Every Claim Checked Before Display

An independent verification agent cross-checks every generated response sentence against your personal lab values and authoritative citations before it ever reaches you.

Educational & Research Demonstration Only: MedTwin is designed strictly as an interactive educational aid grounded in published guidelines (such as ADA Standards of Care, NICE NG28, and WHO HEARTS-D) and machine learning risk scoring.
